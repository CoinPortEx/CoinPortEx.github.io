We're excited to introduce **CoinPort Loyalty Points (CPP)** — the on-chain loyalty rewards program for the CoinPort Exchange community. CPP is now live on **five public EVM blockchains**: Ethereum mainnet (canonical), BNB Smart Chain, Arbitrum One, Polygon, and Avalanche C-Chain —  deployed on 20 May 2026.

CPP is the loyalty token you earn just by being an active CoinPort member. Trade, hold qualifying assets, refer friends, and engage with the platform — CPP accrues automatically against your CoinPort account. When you're ready, you can redeem CPP for free airdrop allocations, trading rebates, priority support, and other member benefits.

## What CPP is — and what it isn't

CPP is a **loyalty rewards token**, not an investment. Here's what that means in practice:

- **You earn CPP through engagement with CoinPort.** There's no CPP sale, no ICO, no fundraising round — and there never has been. The only way to obtain CPP is by being a CoinPort member and using the platform.
- **CPP has no cash value.** You can't convert it to AUD or any other fiat currency. CPP is for redemption inside the CoinPort ecosystem — that's what it's for, by design.
- **CPP isn't traded on secondary markets.** It's a closed-loop loyalty program, similar in spirit to airline miles or retailer rewards points, but with the added transparency of being recorded on public blockchains.
- **CPP isn't a financial product.** It's been designed specifically to operate as a member rewards program under Australian regulation, not as a security, derivative, or speculative asset.

If you've been waiting to "buy CPP" — you haven't missed anything. There's nothing to buy. The only path in is the one that's always been there: be a member, use the platform, earn rewards.

## How you earn CPP

CPP accrues automatically against your CoinPort account through several earning channels:

- **Trading volume.** The primary earning channel. Tiered multipliers reward consistent trading activity, with bonus rates on launchpad assets and altcoin liquidity pairs.
- **Holding qualifying assets.** APR-equivalent CPP accrual on held BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, and CPPT (where applicable), with bonus rates for longer holding commitments.
- **Activity and engagement.** Daily login streaks, profile verification, mobile app activity, and similar member behaviours.
- **Referrals.** A share of trading-fee-equivalent CPP for new members you bring to CoinPort, plus growth bonuses and a small ladder bonus on sub-referrals.
- **Special events.** Trading competitions, beta feature testing, AMAs, and community programs.

Earning rates and limits are published on the CoinPort platform and may change with at least 30 days' notice. Some channels are tier-gated to your member KYC level.

## How you redeem CPP

CPP redeems for benefits within the CoinPort ecosystem:

- **Free airdrop allocations** in CoinPort-listed airdrop events
- **Trading rebates** — fee discounts of 10% to 30% applied to your trades
- **Priority customer support**, beta feature access, API rate-limit boosts
- **Launchpad whitelist allocations**
- **Approved merchandise** and charity donation matching

Redemption is processed inside your CoinPort account, and the underlying CPP is permanently burned at the smart-contract level — so the on-chain supply contracts as the catalog is used.

The redemption catalog evolves over time; the current catalog is always published on the CoinPort platform.

## Live on five chains

This is the bit we're particularly proud of. CPP is deployed as the **same smart contract, at the same address (`0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5`)**, on five target chains:

- **Ethereum mainnet** — canonical reference deployment, live since 18 May 2026. View on [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/token/0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5).
- **BNB Smart Chain** — live. View on [BscScan](https://bscscan.com/address/0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5).
- **Arbitrum One** — live as of 19 May 2026. View on [Arbiscan](https://arbiscan.io/address/0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5).
- **Polygon** — live as of 19 May 2026. View on [PolygonScan](https://polygonscan.com/address/0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5).
- **Avalanche C-Chain** — live as of 19 May 2026. View on [Avascan](https://avascan.info/blockchain/c/token/0x37dAa811B668bf5d19692A7F79579B7BFaaB26A5).

These are sibling deployments — same address, same code, run independently on each chain. CoinPort manages the on-chain supply across all five chains, and your member CPP balance is unified across the entire program. You don't need to think about which chain your CPP "lives on" — that's a CoinPort operational detail.

Going multi-chain was a deliberate design choice. Each supported chain anchors CPP inside a distinct cryptocurrency ecosystem (Ethereum, BNB, Arbitrum, Polygon, Avalanche), so the loyalty program is visible and integrable from wallets, indexers, and tooling native to each one — not only Ethereum-first tooling. And because the contract is deployed at the **same address on every chain**, members, integrators, auditors, and block explorers can recognise CPP by a single canonical identifier regardless of which chain they're querying, which makes tracking, reconciliation, and listing submissions materially simpler.

## Built compliant from day one

CPP is operated by **CoinPort Exchange Pty Ltd**, a registered Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) with AUSTRAC. CPP has been designed from the start to sit alongside CoinPort's existing AUSTRAC AML/CTF Program, with KYC-tiered earning limits, transaction monitoring, and the full set of compliance controls our regulators expect.

For Australian members:

- CPP redemption values may be assessable income under ATO Ruling TR 2005/15. CoinPort provides annual member statements by 14 July.
- The maximum redemption value per member is **AUD $1,000 per calendar year** (GST inclusive).
- Mandatory consumer-law disclosures appear at earning and redemption time.

For the full picture — terms, tax treatment, AML/CTF details, and the regulatory framework — see the [CoinPort Loyalty Points Terms & Conditions](https://www.coinport.com.au) on the CoinPort website, alongside the [CPP White Paper](https://www.coinport.com.au) for the technical and program-design detail.
