## **General Questions**

### **1. What is CoinPort?**
CoinPort is an Australian owned and operated platform for buying, selling and swapping crypto with Australian dollars. We're registered with AUSTRAC as a virtual asset service provider. There are no trading fees, the price you see includes our small spread, and you can reach our team by live chat or email. Our help bot answers around the clock; our team replies Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, Melbourne time.

### **2. Is CoinPort regulated in Australia?**
Yes. CoinPort Pty Ltd is registered with AUSTRAC as a Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) — registration 100633359 and complies with AUSTRAC's requirements for anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorism financing (CTF).

### **3. Which cryptocurrencies does CoinPort support?**
We support the major cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC) and BNB. For a full list of supported assets, visit our <a href="https://www.coinport.com.au/research" target="_parent">Research</a> page.

### **4. Can I use CoinPort outside Australia?**
Currently, CoinPort primarily serves Australian residents. Some features may be restricted for users outside Australia due to regulatory requirements.

### **5. How do I contact CoinPort support?**
You can reach us via:
- **Porter AI Chat Bot** (answers common questions at any time)
- **Live Chat** with our team (during Melbourne business hours)
- **Email:** support@coinport.com.au

We don't operate a manned phone line — chat and email are the fastest ways to
reach us.

## **Using CoinPort**

### **1. Where can I see my balance?**
Your **Home** page shows what your whole account is worth in Australian dollars, split into crypto and AUD. For each coin on its own, open **Wallets**: every coin you hold is listed with its balance, and choosing one shows its full details. **Portfolio** shows the same holdings as a chart, so you can see how they've moved over time.

### **2. What's the difference between Available and Locked?**
**Available** is what you can use right now: to buy, sell, swap or withdraw. **Locked** is set aside for something in progress, such as an open order or a withdrawal that's being sent. It moves back to Available when the order fills or is cancelled, or once the withdrawal completes.

### **3. How do I buy crypto?**
Open **Buy**, choose the coin and enter how many Australian dollars you'd like to spend. You'll see the live price and how much crypto you'll get before you confirm. Pay by PayID from a bank account in your own name, and the crypto lands in your wallet in minutes.

### **4. How do I sell crypto?**
Open **Sell**, choose the coin and the amount, and confirm. The Australian dollars are paid to your own bank account by PayID, usually within minutes. If the coin isn't in your CoinPort wallet yet, the page shows you where to send it first.

### **5. How do I swap one coin for another?**
Open **Swap**, choose the coin you're swapping from and the coin you want, and enter an amount. You'll see the live rate before you confirm, and the new coin appears in your wallet straight away.

### **6. Where do I find my PayID or bank details for depositing AUD?**
Open **Wallets**, choose **AUD** and select **Deposit**. Your own PayID and BSB/account number are shown there, with a copy button for each. Send from a bank account in your own name.

### **7. Where can I see my past transactions?**
Open **History** from the menu. Deposits, withdrawals, transfers and trades each have their own tab, and you can filter by coin, status and date, or download a CSV for your records.

### **8. How do I send crypto to another CoinPort customer?**
Use **Transfer**. Find the person by their CoinPort username, UID or email, choose the coin and amount, and confirm with your 2FA code. Transfers between CoinPort accounts are free and arrive instantly.

## **Account Setup & Verification**

### **1. How do I create a CoinPort account?**
1. Visit <a href="https://www.coinport.com.au" target="_parent">CoinPort’s website</a> and click **"Sign Up."**
2. Enter your email, create a password, and agree to the terms.
3. Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
4. Complete identity verification (KYC) to unlock full account features.

### **2. Why do I need to verify my identity (KYC)?**
As a business registered with AUSTRAC, we must comply with Australian AML/CTF laws. Verification keeps your account secure and helps prevent fraud.

### **3. What documents are required for verification?**
Just one photo ID and a selfie — there is no proof-of-address step, so you don't need a utility bill or bank statement.
- **Australian residents:** Australian passport or driver's licence
- **Everyone else:** passport

You'll scan the document with your camera and take a short live selfie so we can match it to the photo. A phone camera in good light works best.

### **4. How long does verification take?**
Most verifications complete within **1–5 minutes**, and often much faster than that. If a manual review is needed it can take up to 24 hours. Delays usually mean the document photo was unclear.

### **5. Can I have multiple CoinPort accounts?**
No, each user is allowed only one verified account. Duplicate accounts may be suspended.

### **6. How do I reset my password?**
Use **"Forgot your password?"** on the sign-in page and we'll email you a reset link. Support will never ask you for your password — if anyone does, it isn't us.

### **7. I've lost my phone or can't get my 2FA code**
2FA can't be reset over chat, because that's exactly what an attacker would ask for. Email support@coinport.com.au from the address on your account and we'll take you through an identity check.

### **8. How do I close my account?**
Withdraw any remaining balance first, then contact support and we'll close it. We're required to keep transaction and identity records for seven years under AML/CTF law, so closing an account doesn't erase that history.

## **Deposits & Withdrawals**

### **1. How do I deposit funds into my CoinPort account?**
**Australian dollars**
1. Open **Wallets** and choose **AUD**.
2. Select **Deposit** to see your own PayID and BSB/account number.
3. Send the money from a bank account in your own name. By PayID it usually arrives within a minute.

**Crypto**
1. Open **Wallets** and choose the coin.
2. Select **Deposit** and pick the network you're sending on.
3. Copy the address (or scan the QR code) and send from your other wallet or exchange. Always send on the same network as the address shown.

### **2. Are there deposit fees?**
- **AUD Deposits:** Free
- **Crypto Deposits:** Network fees may apply (varies by blockchain)

### **3. How long do deposits take?**
- **AUD:** Usually instant via Osko/PayID (under a minute), 24/7 including weekends
- **Crypto:** Depends on network congestion (Bitcoin: ~10–30 mins, Ethereum: ~1–5 mins)

### **4. How do I withdraw funds?**
You'll need 2FA turned on first. Withdrawals are confirmed with your 2FA code.

**Australian dollars to your bank**
1. Open **Wallets**, choose **AUD** and select **Withdraw**.
2. Choose your saved bank account, or add one. It must be a PayID in your own name.
3. Enter the amount and confirm with your 2FA code. Most AUD withdrawals arrive within minutes.

**Crypto to another wallet**
1. Open **Wallets**, choose the coin and select **Withdraw**.
2. Choose a saved address, or add one and pick its network.
3. Enter the amount and confirm with your 2FA code. We pay the network fee, so the full amount is sent.

To turn crypto into dollars in your bank in one go, use **Sell** instead.

### **5. Are there withdrawal limits?**
Withdrawal limits depend on your verification level and are shown on the withdrawal page in your account. For higher limits, contact support.

### **6. Can I deposit cash or pay by card?**
No. Deposits are bank transfer (PayID or BSB/account) or cryptocurrency only. The bank account you send from must be in your own name.

### **7. Why am I being asked where my crypto came from?**
Incoming crypto deposits may prompt a short source-of-funds question — your own wallet, your account at another exchange, or sent by someone else. It's an AUSTRAC Travel Rule requirement, takes about 30 seconds, and clears the notice on your deposit.

Adding a crypto withdrawal address asks a similar question about who controls the destination wallet. You answer that once per saved address.

### **8. I sent crypto on the wrong network. Can I get it back?**
Sometimes, but never assume so — blockchain transfers can't be reversed and recovery isn't always technically possible. We may attempt it where the deposit was over **AUD $3,000** and your account is fully verified. A **non-refundable attempt fee of AUD $1,500** applies and is payable in advance, whether or not the recovery succeeds. Contact support with the transaction hash and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth trying.

## **Trading & Fees**

### **1. What trading pairs are available on CoinPort?**
We offer AUD trading pairs, such as BTC/AUD and ETH/AUD. You can also swap one coin for another on our Swap page.

### **2. What are CoinPort’s trading fees?**
- There are no commissions or fees for crypto orders or trades on the CoinPort exchange platform. We try to make CoinPort as simple and transparent as possible. The price quoted is the price you receive.

### **3. What order types does CoinPort support?**
We support:
- **Market Orders**: buy or sell straight away at the current price
- **Limit Orders**: set the price you want, and the order fills when the market reaches it

Stop-loss and take-profit orders are not available.

### **4. Can I use APIs for automated trading?**
Yes, CoinPort provides a **REST API** and **WebSocket API** for developers.

### **5. Do you offer staking, margin or futures?**
Not at the moment. CoinPort is spot trading only — no margin, leverage, futures or short selling, and no staking or interest products.
